# Status: p4/transcribe (Part 2, step 4)

Transcription as `docs/research/transcription.md` and D-087 describe it, plus voice notes on comments. Decisions D-117 to D-120. Branch `p4/transcribe`, merged with main at `94ad5ac` (main had not moved).

## Built

- **Providers** (`apps/server/src/lib/server/transcribe/`): `TRANSCRIBE_PROVIDER=local` (whisper.cpp, default), `openai` (any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, default `gpt-transcribe` at `https://api.openai.com/v1`) or `none`. The old names `whispercpp`, `openai-compatible` and `STT_*` still work. `TRANSCRIBE_API_KEY` falls back to `OPENAI_API_KEY`; a key alone does not switch provider. Settings: `config.ts`.
- **Chunked timestamps** for text-only models: decode once to 16 kHz PCM, adaptive pause detector, chunks of at most `TRANSCRIBE_CHUNK_SECONDS` (30) cut at pauses (hard cut with one second of overlap when nobody pauses, repeated words dropped), silent chunks not sent, 32 kbps Opus per chunk, parallel requests (`TRANSCRIBE_CONCURRENCY`, 4), WebVTT built on the server with sentence cues spread over the voiced time and offset by the chunk start. Native WebVTT for Whisper-style models, chunked with offsets above 24 MB; `TRANSCRIBE_TIMESTAMPS=auto` falls back to chunked on the 400 "not compatible" answer.
- **Retries**: four tries per request on 408, 409, 429 (not an exhausted quota), 5xx and network errors, exponential backoff and `Retry-After`; the job's five attempts on top.
- **Guards**: `TRANSCRIBE_MAX_MINUTES` (60) for every provider; `TRANSCRIBE_MONTHLY_MINUTES` for hosted audio, reserved before sending in `instance_setting` (`transcribe.usage.YYYY-MM`), settled to billed seconds, refunded on failure. New transcript status `skipped` with the reason. No migration.
- **Voice notes on comments**: asset kind `voice`, one per comment; the comment queues a transcript (stream `voice:<assetId>`). The comment DTO has `voice` (audio URL, transcript status, language, text, reason). The note's VTT is at `media/transcript-<assetId>` and `GET /api/v1/recordings/:id/transcript?asset=<id>`. MCP `list_comments` adds `voiceNote.transcript`, and `get_transcript` reports skipped transcripts and says the text is data. The viewer's comment composer has a microphone button that records, previews and attaches the note, and the comment shows the audio with its transcript. A finished job pushes the change to open viewers.
- **Traces**: a trace's voice track is the capture's `voice` stream, transcribed by the same job (no trace-specific code; `p4/trace` was not merged here).
- **Fixtures**: `apps/server/fixtures/transcribe/fleurs-sw_ke-1999.webm` (18.0 s, 70 KB) and `fleurs-en_us-1904.webm` (10.6 s, 41 KB) with `ATTRIBUTION.md` (FLEURS, CC BY 4.0).
- **Docs**: `docs/configuration.md` "Transcription" (every variable, size, cost, failures, timestamps, privacy, Swahili and English quality per provider), `docs/architecture.md` section 12, `docs/dev.md` (tests, live test command), `.env.example`, `docker-compose.yml` (every `TRANSCRIBE_*` passed through, default `local`).

## How it was verified

- `apps/server/test/transcribe.test.ts` (31 tests, no network, no ffmpeg): settings and aliases; pause detection on synthetic speech, a -60 dB microphone included; chunk plans; cue building, long-sentence splits, overlap removal, VTT round trip; recorded `gpt-transcribe` JSON, the 400 that refuses `vtt` and a `whisper-1` VTT body; retries (429 with `Retry-After`, 503, network errors) and no retry on 401, the refused format or `insufficient_quota`; chunked, native and over-24-MB paths with offsets; the length cap, monthly cap, refund on failure and free local provider.
- `apps/server/test/transcribe-chunker.test.ts` (3 tests, ffmpeg): a 2 minute file built at test time from the two fixtures, alternating with 0.8 s and 2 s pauses and one Swahili span 30 dB down. Every cut sits at least 15 dB below the loudest frame of the 3 s around it, most cuts land on the inserted pauses, the quiet span stays speech, and a re-encoded slice keeps its length.
- `apps/server/test/transcribe.int.test.ts` (6 tests): throwaway Postgres database, Garage under its own prefix, a local stand-in for the endpoint. Voice note upload, comment, job, transcript in the comment and at `media/transcript-<id>`; a MediaRecorder-style note with no duration header; the capture's own voice track; the monthly cap skipping the next note without a request; provider `none`; one note per comment and audio types only.
- `apps/server/test/transcribe.live.test.ts`: run once with the project key (`TRANSCRIBE_LIVE_TEST=1`). Swahili: detected `sw`, CER 24.2% against the digits reference before the spelled-out year was accepted as a reference variant; English: detected `en`, CER 1.0%; 29 billed seconds. A second run with the variant reference: Swahili CER 2.8%, English 1.0%. Spend for this package, live test plus two e2e voice notes of about 6 s: under $0.01.
- `e2e/tests/viewer/voice-note.spec.ts`: Chromium's fake microphone plays the Swahili fixture into the composer, the note is sent, the comment shows the audio and the transcript arrives over the live stream ("Katika meka yote elfu moja mia tisa sitini.", `gpt-transcribe`, 6 s billed). Ran against `pnpm dev` on :3304 (database `toolbar_p4_transcribe`, `S3_KEY_PREFIX=p4-transcribe/`, `TRANSCRIBE_PROVIDER=openai`) with the viewer `comments` and `media` suites: 25 passed, 1 skipped (paused AI chapters).
- Local whisper.cpp through the new code against the compose `whisper` service on the English fixture: one request, 4.6 s, correct text, not counted against the monthly cap.
- `pnpm -r --workspace-concurrency=2 build`, `test` (server 221 passed, 7 skipped) and `check` pass.
